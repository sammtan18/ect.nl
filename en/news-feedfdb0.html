5 September 2021

Largest Container Vessel in the World at ECT

Saturday 4 September 4 2021, the Ever Ace of shipping line Evergreen Marine Company moored at the ECT Delta terminal in the Amazonehaven in Rotterdam. The maiden call of this vessel is extra special, because the Ever Ace is stowed all around with the green Evergreen containers. The Ever Ace was delivered in July from the Samsung Heavy Industries yard in South Korea. The ship has a length of 400 m, a beam of 61 m and a capacity of 23,992 TEU. This makes the Ever Ace the largest container vessel in the world.

Rotterdam, August 18, 2021 – Ten new hybrid Straddle Carriers arrived today at the ECT Delta terminal (photo).
