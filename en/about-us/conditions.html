Code of Conduct

It is not just our environment that imposes high standards on the way we work: we impose these high standards on ourselves. To supply quality and work carefully, we need to make safety and integrity our highest priorities. At ECT we put safety – of people and the environment – top of the list. Always. We act according to the applicable Code of Conduct.

Report undesirable behavior and Whistleblower policy 

ECT values that people treat each other respectfully. This means that sexual intimidation, bullying, swearing, aggression, violence and discrimination are not accepted. However, if you have experienced undesirable behavior or have been treated unfairly by an ECT employee, you can report this.

Undesirable behavior includes, among other things:

  • Aggression and violence
  • (Sexual) harassment
  • Bullying
  • Discrimination

Additionally, anyone who encounters ECT in a work-related context can also use this reporting point to make whistleblower reports regarding situations involving for example: misconduct, fraud or other irregularities within ECT. Your report will be treated confidentially. More information can be found here.

This does NOT include complaints regarding the operational process, such as waiting times or disruptions.

ECT has chosen to accommodate the reporting process at an external party: Vertrouwen in Werk agency. This office can be reached via 085 – 888 0 886 from Monday to Friday between 8:30 am and 5:00 pm. They will further discuss the further handling of your complaint or whistleblower report with you.
