Intermodal Services

    • The synchromodal service provider of Europe

    Hutchison Ports Europe Intermodal (HPEI), ECT offers shipping lines, logistics service providers and shippers an extensive network of highly frequent rail and inland barge services. These services directly connect the deepsea terminals in Rotterdam with a growing number of strategically located inland terminals in the European market.

    Because HPEI is a trendsetter in synchromodal transport, it can offer its customers the best possible solution in terms of price, quality and sustainability. From various modes of transport – rail, inland shipping, truck – the best option for that particular moment is always selected for the customer. If necessary (for reasons of speed or because of obstructions, for example), the mode of transport can easily be switched up to the very last moment. Customers are assured optimum flexibility.

    Furthermore, HPEI offers customers numerous additional services, such as transport without customs documents, storage, empty depot services and delivery right up to the doorstep.
