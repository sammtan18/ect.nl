21 November 2019

IEX Service at the ECT Delta Terminal

From the end of this week, the IEX service of Hapag Lloyd, Cosco, OOCL, ONE and Yang Ming will call at ECT's ECT Delta terminal. The IEX service is the Southeast India - Europe Express service launched this fall. This service is the only service that connects Southeast India with Europe. THE IEX service will call at the ECT Delta terminal on a weekly basis. The first ship to call at the ECT Delta terminal is the AL Rawdah on November 23, 2019.

Maidencall Ever Glory at ECT
